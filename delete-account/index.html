KAKI Privacy Delete account KAKI Active · Account control Delete your KAKI account You can request permanent deletion even if you have uninstalled KAKI or cannot sign in. Last updated 3 September 2026 How to request deletion Use the button below to email KAKI Active. Send the request from the email address registered to your KAKI account whenever possible. Keep the subject line Delete my KAKI account and include your KAKI display name. We may ask for limited additional information to verify account ownership. Never send us your password. Permanent account deletion Send your request The email is pre-addressed and includes a short request template. You can review it before sending. Email KAKI Active kaki.app.sg@gmail.com What happens next We will acknowledge your request and verify that you control the account. We aim to complete a verified request within 30 days and will send confirmation when processing is complete. Deleting the app from your phone does not delete your KAKI account. Signing out or temporarily disabling access also does not count as account deletion. Data covered by the request Deletion covers the KAKI account and information associated with it, including: account and profile information; personal activity logs, preferences, and season goals; squad memberships, invitations, RSVPs, votes, reactions, and responses; Board content and answers associated with your account; and photos and videos you contributed where they remain linked to your account. Where necessary to preserve a squad’s shared seasonal record, limited information may be kept only in a de-identified form that no longer identifies you. Limited records may also be retained when reasonably required for security, fraud prevention, dispute resolution, or legal compliance. Protected residual copies may remain in routine backups until overwritten. Questions or corrections If you want to correct information instead of deleting the entire account, explain what you need in an email to kaki.app.sg@gmail.com.
