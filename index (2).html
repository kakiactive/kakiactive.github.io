Privacy Policy

This policy explains how the KAKI mobile app collects, uses, protects, and deletes information.

Effective 3 September 2026 · Last updated 3 September 2026

Contents Who we are Information collected How we use it Visibility and sharing Retention and deletion Security Your choices Contact

1. Who we are

KAKI is an activity and squad coordination app operated by KAKI Active. In this policy, “KAKI”, “we”, “us”, and “our” refer to KAKI Active and the KAKI mobile app.

Privacy enquiries and account-deletion requests may be sent to kaki.app.sg@gmail.com.

2. Information we collect

We collect information you provide and information created when you use KAKI’s features:

  • Account and profile information: email address, user ID, display name, activity preferences, onboarding motivation, and season goals.
  • Squad and location information: squad membership and role, squad name, selected Singapore planning area or MRT area, venue names, mission dates and times, invitations, and RSVP status. KAKI does not request your device’s precise GPS location.
  • Activity and fitness information: selected activities, personal activity logs, duration, place names, season goals, mission attendance, participation, and season records.
  • Photos and videos: official mission check-in photos and optional photos or short videos contributed to squad memories.
  • Board and community content: pins, questions, answers, notices, tips, wins, polls, votes, reactions, links, and other responses you choose to submit.
  • Technical and security information: authentication and session information, timestamps, and limited network or server logs processed to operate, secure, and troubleshoot the service.
No device location tracking.

KAKI stores places that users select or type, such as a planning area, MRT area, or venue. The current app configuration does not request Android fine-location or background-location permission.

3. How we use information

We use information to:

  • create, authenticate, and manage your KAKI account;
  • show your profile, activity passport, squads, and season progress;
  • coordinate missions, invitations, attendance, RSVPs, and squad leadership;
  • build squad memories and Season Books;
  • operate Board polls, questions, reactions, and unread indicators;
  • protect KAKI, enforce access controls, prevent misuse, and investigate technical problems;
  • respond to support, privacy, and account-deletion requests; and
  • comply with applicable legal obligations.

We do not use KAKI account data for targeted advertising and we do not sell personal information.

4. Visibility and sharing

KAKI is designed around small squads. Information you submit to a squad may be visible to the relevant squad members. This can include your display name, activity, RSVP, attendance, Board content, reactions, photos, videos, and contributions shown in a Season Book.

We use service providers to operate KAKI. Supabase provides authentication, database, and private media-storage services. Email providers process messages you send to our support address. These providers process information on our behalf and under their own security and privacy commitments.

We may disclose information when reasonably necessary to comply with law, protect users, investigate misuse, or protect the rights and safety of KAKI Active and others.

We do not rent or sell your personal information. We do not share KAKI account data with advertising networks or data brokers.

5. Retention and deletion

We keep account information while your account is active and retain feature data for as long as needed to operate KAKI’s squads, missions, current seasons, and Season Books.

Board items disappear from the normal Board when they expire. Limited backend records may remain for integrity, abuse prevention, and troubleshooting until they are removed through account deletion or routine maintenance. Shared seasonal records may be retained in de-identified form so they no longer identify a deleted member.

After a verified account-deletion request, we aim to complete deletion within 30 days. We delete or de-identify information associated with the account, including profile information, personal activity data, memberships, responses, and personal media contributions. Limited information may be retained where reasonably required for security, fraud prevention, dispute resolution, or legal compliance. Any residual copies in routine backups remain protected until overwritten.

See Delete your KAKI account for the request process.

6. Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational safeguards, including encrypted network connections, authentication, database access controls, row-level access policies, and private media-storage buckets. Access to squad data is restricted according to membership and feature rules.

No service can guarantee absolute security. If you believe your account or information has been compromised, contact us promptly.

7. Your choices

  • You can choose whether to add optional activity notes, Board content, photos, and videos.
  • You can replace or remove eligible memory contributions during the period shown in the app.
  • You may ask us to access, correct, or delete information associated with your account.
  • You may permanently delete your account through the process on our account-deletion page.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when KAKI’s features or data practices change. We will update the date at the top of this page and provide additional notice when appropriate.
